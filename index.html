Button Hover Lab

Thirty-five hover animations, actually built — hover, tab and press all of them. A monochrome base, so each one carries any brand tone. Four house rules apply to every single one:

Instant responseThe press lands on pointer-down, not on release.
InterruptibleHover out halfway — the animation turns smoothly from wherever it is.
In one way, out anotherThe exit is designed, never just the entrance rewound.
Reduced motionEverything falls back to plain colour and opacity changes.
